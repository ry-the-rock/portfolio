About Me

Hello! My name is Rylie Hansen. I am currently a senior computer science and math major at St. Olaf College in Northfield, Minnesota (Go Oles!). When I am not ruining my eyesight staring at a computer, I enjoy taking walks in nature, playing the drums, and snuggling with my precious cat, Mr. Bean. I intend on pursuing a PhD in computing education in the fall of 2027, with a specific interest in accessibility and interdisciplinary studies, to help further my dreams of becoming a CS professor. You can reach me by email at rylie.e.hansen@gmail.com if you would like to chat. Thanks for stopping by!
